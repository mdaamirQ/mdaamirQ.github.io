Human–AI collaboration

Knowing when to ask: uncertainty in human–AI collaboration

Read abstract

This illustrative abstract outlines a study of how uncertainty signals influence delegation between a person and an AI assistant. The proposed work compares concise confidence cues with explanations of uncertainty, and examines task success, correction behavior, and perceived control. The goal is to identify when asking for human input improves a shared workflow.

Learning & generalization

Beyond the benchmark: studying generalization in context

Read abstract

This illustrative abstract describes an evaluation framework for testing representations across changing task conditions. It proposes controlled shifts in input structure and task requirements, paired with qualitative analysis of model failures. The intended contribution is a clearer account of when a model’s apparent capabilities transfer to new settings.
