Learning & generalization

Understanding what models learn—and how that knowledge travels to new settings.

The question

What makes a learned representation useful beyond its training data?

Research approach

This theme connects model behavior to the structure of training data. The aim is to understand where generalization succeeds, where it breaks, and how to measure the difference.

Machine learning · Representation learning

What I’m working on
